    Dog walking, dog sitting and pet sitting across Norfolk and Suffolk

    Pawfect Pet Care is a local, DBS-checked and fully insured pet care service based in Rackheath, Norwich. We provide professional dog walking, dog sitting, pet sitting, cat sitting, puppy visits, home visits, pop-in visits, house sitting and feeding visits across Norfolk and most of Suffolk.

    Dog walking in Norwich, Rackheath, Salhouse, Wroxham and across Norfolk

    Our dog walking services include solo dog walking, small-group dog walking, puppy walking, senior dog walking, nervous dog walking and reactive dog walking. Whether you need a regular dog walker while you are at work or one-off walks for a busy week, we tailor each walk to your dog's age, energy and behaviour.

    Pet sitting, dog sitting and cat sitting in Norfolk and Suffolk

    Our pet sitting services include dog sitting, cat sitting, small-animal sitting and in-home pet sitting so your pets stay in their own familiar surroundings. We offer feeding visits, medication visits, pop-in visits, home visits and house sitting so cats, dogs, rabbits and other pets keep their normal routine while you are away.

    Puppy visits, puppy care and puppy walking

    Puppy visits and puppy care are available for young dogs who are not yet ready for full walks. We provide toilet breaks, feeding, play, socialisation and gentle lead training so your puppy builds confidence.

    Home visits, pop-in visits and house sitting

    Home visits and pop-in visits are ideal for pets who prefer to stay home. We provide feeding, fresh water, medication, litter tray cleaning, toilet breaks, play and company. House sitting is available for longer trips so your home stays occupied and your pets stay relaxed.

    Dog walking and pet sitting in Suffolk

    We cover most of Suffolk including Lowestoft, Beccles, Bungay, Halesworth, Saxmundham, Eye, Stowmarket and surrounding Suffolk towns and villages.
